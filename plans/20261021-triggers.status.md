# Status: triggers (ADR 0018, slice 5)

Created 2026-10-21. Status: open.
Task file: [task](20261021-triggers.task.md).
Specification: [ADR 0018](../docs/adr-0018-surface-sql.md), D5.

## Progress

- 2026-10-21: the task file, from native probes and the measurement of the cases that create
  a trigger.

- 2026-10-21: the model's trigger definitions. The syntax has `CREATE TRIGGER`, `DROP TRIGGER`,
  the body statements and `RAISE` (`Syntax.lean`, `SyntaxExpr.lean`, with a proved
  `DecidableEq` of `Syntax.Expr` in `SyntaxEquality.lean`). Triggers are catalog objects with a
  namespace of their own (`CatalogObjects.lean`, `Catalog.foldedNames` without them,
  `CatalogStep.appendTrigger`), resolve with SQLite's errors (`TriggerDefinitions.lean`, laws in
  `TriggerDefinitionSpec.lean`), and DROP TABLE drops them. `RAISE` outside a trigger is
  SQLite's prepare error. Writes do not run triggers yet: a write on a catalog with a trigger,
  and DROP TABLE with foreign-key actions on one, fail the support check (`TriggerExamples.lean`).
- 2026-10-21: the frontend (`belay/sqlite/syntax_triggers.py`, `RAISE` in `syntax_queries.py`,
  DROP TRIGGER in `syntax_drops.py`, tests in `tests/syntax_triggers_test.py`). It refuses
  TEMP, INSTEAD OF, schema names, qualified body tables, INDEXED BY, UPDATE OR, UPDATE FROM,
  INSERT ... SELECT, RETURNING and a subquery in WHEN.

- 2026-10-21: trigger programs in the resolution of writes. A write's `foreignKeys` depth became
  `rules : Option WriteRules` (depth, keys, programs; `TriggerProgram.lean`). `NEW` and `OLD`
  are trigger sources of the scope (`ScopeSource.trigger`, `TriggerRow` and SQLite's qualified
  lookup order in `ScopeNames.lean`, split from `Scope.lean`). Bodies resolve with the
  statement resolvers (`TriggerBodies.lean`, `RAISE` in `TriggerRaise.lean`), the closure over
  body writes, foreign-key actions and REPLACE deletes is `TriggerClosure.lean`, and
  `TriggerRules.lean` orders the programs newest first and restricts a `BEFORE UPDATE` or
  `BEFORE DELETE` program that writes its own table. New restrictions: `raise-form`,
  `trigger-form`, `trigger-writes-own-table`, `schema-trigger-order` (with examples in
  `restriction_examples.py`, checked in Nix). Examples in `TriggerProgramExamples.lean`.
